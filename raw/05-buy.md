> **Check the wardrobe before checking out.** Four of the five look formulas improved in one week and none of it came from this plan — three came from recording garments already owned, one from an £80 belt.
>
> **The answer to a gap keeps being "you already own it".** The instances so far:
>
> | When | The gap | What it actually was |
> |---|---|---|
> | The fine-knit constraint | No roll neck, no fine knit at the collar | #171, #172, #173 — owned, unrecorded |
> | The tan outer layer | No brown or tan smart-casual outer layer | #152 — owned, and it narrowed #170 rather than closing it |
> | **30 Aug 2026** | **The client-day mode rule asks for a white pocket square and no look could meet it.** It was about to go on this plan | **#176 — owned, unrecorded** |
>
> **The pattern: a gap surfaced by a rule is far more likely to be phantom than a gap surfaced by a look.** A look fails concretely — the trouser is light, the sock is black, and no unrecorded garment makes that go away. A rule fails against the *record*, so it fires just as loudly when the item exists and simply isn't in the CSV. **Before a rule-surfaced gap joins this plan, search the wardrobe for the thing itself, not the row.**
