About the flat-lays

These are schematics, not photographs — each garment is drawn as its type and filled with the hex value recorded for it in 03-wardrobe.csv. They are here to show value contrast, which is the rule that outranks colour: read the L / M / D strip under each one. L + D passes. All-M fails.

Colours are only as accurate as the CSV, and several were re-derived by eye during the 22 August sweep rather than measured. Treat the shapes as diagrams and the hexes as close-but-not-exact.

Product links. Where a garment could be found online, the item line carries a link. see it is the actual product; closest is the nearest current equivalent from the same maker, because the original is discontinued or an older season. 20 of the 79 items in this set are linked. The rest are unlabelled (#110, the ties, #163), long discontinued, or too generic to identify with confidence — and a wrong link is worse than none.
